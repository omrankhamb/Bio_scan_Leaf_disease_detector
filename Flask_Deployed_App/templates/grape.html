🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

grape

Downy Mildew

Description: Yellow oil-like spots on upper leaf surface, white powdery growth on underside.

Remedy: Improve air circulation, apply copper sulfate fungicides, remove infected leaves, crop rotation.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery coating on leaves, shoots, berries, and flower buds.

Remedy: Apply sulfur fungicides, ensure good air circulation, prune dense foliage, and maintain vine health.

Black Rot

Description: Dark brown lesions on leaves with concentric rings, berries turn black and shrivel.

Remedy: Remove affected berries and shoots, apply Bordeaux mixture, improve canopy ventilation.

Anthracnose

Description: Dark spots on leaves with red borders, sunken lesions on shoots and fruit.

Remedy: Prune infected canes, apply fungicides, remove fallen leaves, and practice crop rotation.

Leaf Spot

Description: Brown or tan spots on grape leaves with reddish margins, spots enlarge and coalesce.

Remedy: Apply copper fungicides, remove infected leaves, improve air circulation, and sanitize tools.

Ripe Rot

Description: Soft rot of ripe berries with brown discoloration, white mold growth on fruit.

Remedy: Remove infected berries, apply fungicides at veraison, improve air flow, and maintain canopy health.

Grapevine Yellows

Description: Yellowing of leaves, reduced growth, and poor fruit development in affected plants.

Remedy: Remove infected vines, control insect vectors, use healthy propagating material, and practice roguing.

Botrytis Bunch Rot

Description: Gray mold on berries causing soft rot, especially during humid conditions.

Remedy: Thin flower clusters, improve air circulation, apply fungicides, and harvest at optimal maturity.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Health Benefits

Resveratrol

Benefit: Powerful antioxidant found in grape skins, supports heart health and reduces inflammation.

Recommendation: Consume 1-2 cups of fresh grapes daily or 5 oz of red wine for maximum resveratrol intake.

Vitamin C

Benefit: Boosts immune system, aids collagen synthesis, and protects against free radicals.

Recommendation: One cup of grapes provides 15% of daily Vitamin C requirements; combine with citrus for enhanced absorption.

Antioxidants

Benefit: Quercetin and anthocyanins reduce oxidative stress and support brain health.

Recommendation: Dark purple/red grapes contain highest antioxidant levels; consume 150-200g daily.

Potassium

Benefit: Regulates blood pressure, supports heart function, and maintains fluid balance.

Recommendation: One cup of grapes provides 10% of daily potassium; ideal for athletes and heart health.

Fiber

Benefit: Improves digestive health, aids weight management, and stabilizes blood sugar.

Recommendation: Eat grapes with skin for optimal fiber content; aim for 1-2 servings per day.

Polyphenols

Benefit: Anti-inflammatory compounds that support cardiovascular and neurological health.

Recommendation: Fresh grapes, grape juice (unsweetened), and red wine are excellent polyphenol sources.
