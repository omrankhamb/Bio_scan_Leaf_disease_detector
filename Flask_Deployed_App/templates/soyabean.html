🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Soybean

Asian Soybean Rust

Description: Yellow flecks on leaves that develop into large pustules producing spores under humid conditions.

Remedy: Use resistant varieties, apply systemic fungicides at first signs, and monitor regularly.

Frogeye Leaf Spot

Description: Circular lesions with dark borders on leaves and pods, caused by Cercospora sojina.

Remedy: Use crop rotation, resistant cultivars, and fungicides when necessary.

Bacterial Blight

Description: Water-soaked spots that turn tan with a yellow halo; can defoliate under wet conditions.

Remedy: Use pathogen-free seed, avoid overhead irrigation, and manage crop residue.

Septoria Brown Spot

Description: Small brown spots on lower leaves, often causing premature defoliation in dense canopies.

Remedy: Improve airflow, rotate crops, and apply fungicides if severe.

Sudden Death Syndrome (SDS)

Description: Root infection leading to interveinal chlorosis, necrosis and plant collapse.

Remedy: Plant tolerant varieties, improve drainage, and rotate crops to non-hosts.

Phytophthora Root/Stem Rot

Description: Seedling damping-off or stem rot in saturated soils; leads to plant death.

Remedy: Improve seedbed drainage, use treated seed, and avoid planting in poorly drained areas.

Downy Mildew

Description: Pale lesions and downy growth on leaf undersides under cool, moist conditions.

Remedy: Promote air flow, avoid overhead watering, and apply fungicides when needed.

Soybean Mosaic Virus

Description: Mottled leaves, stunting and reduced vigor; virus vectored by aphids.

Remedy: Use virus-free seed, control aphids, and remove infected plants.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Neerley 6

Purpose: Targeted soybean fertilizer formulation (Neerley 6) to support leaf health and yield.

Application: Follow manufacturer's label; typically applied as soil or foliar feed during vegetative growth.

Balanced NPK

Purpose: Provides N-P-K for robust vegetative and pod development.

Application: Apply according to soil test; split doses reduce disease stress.

Micronutrient Mix

Benefit: Supplies trace elements (Fe, Zn, Mn, B) that improve plant resilience and disease resistance.

Application: Use foliar sprays or soil chelates per recommendations.

Compost Tea (Organic Biofertilizer)

Benefit: Boosts beneficial soil microbes, improves nutrient availability and plant vigor.

Application: Apply as a soil drench or foliar feed during early vegetative stages; make fresh tea and use within 24 hours.

Rhizobium Inoculant

Benefit: Introduces nitrogen-fixing bacteria to improve nodulation and reduce fertilizer needs.

Application: Coat seed with inoculant at planting (follow label rates) for best establishment of symbiosis.

Seaweed Extract (Biostimulant)

Benefit: Promotes root growth, stress tolerance and improved flowering/fruit set.

Application: Apply as foliar spray or soil drench during vegetative and pre-flowering stages per product directions.
