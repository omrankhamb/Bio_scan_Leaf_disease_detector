🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Squash

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery patches on leaves that reduce photosynthesis and vigor.

Remedy: Improve air circulation, remove infected tissue, and apply sulfur or bicarbonate treatments.

Downy Mildew

Description: Yellowing and downy growth on leaf undersides in cool, wet weather.

Remedy: Avoid overhead watering, improve drainage and apply recommended fungicides.

Anthracnose

Description: Sunken lesions on fruit and leaves that can cause rot and defoliation.

Remedy: Remove infected parts, practice sanitation and apply fungicides at flowering/fruit set.

Bacterial Wilt

Description: Sudden wilting and collapse due to bacteria spread by cucumber beetles.

Remedy: Control beetle vectors, remove infected plants and practice crop rotation.

Fusarium Wilt

Description: Yellowing and wilting caused by soilborne Fusarium species; plants may decline gradually.

Remedy: Use resistant varieties, solarize soil and rotate with non-host crops.

Mosaic Virus

Description: Mottled or distorted leaves and stunted growth; vectored by aphids and other pests.

Remedy: Use virus-free seed, control aphids and rogue infected plants.

Squash Vine Borer Damage

Description: Sudden wilting of vines mid-season caused by borer larvae inside stems.

Remedy: Monitor for eggs, use row covers, remove and destroy infested vines, and apply appropriate insecticides.

Gummy Stem Blight

Description: Cankers and gummy exudate on stems, lesions on leaves and fruit; caused by Didymella bryoniae.

Remedy: Remove infected vines, rotate crops, and apply fungicides when conditions favor disease.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Nearley 6

Purpose: Targeted squash fertilizer to support leaf health and fruit set.

Application: Follow label instructions; typically applied as soil or foliar feed during vegetative growth.

Balanced NPK

Purpose: General N-P-K for vine vigor and fruit development.

Application: Apply per soil test and split applications to reduce disease susceptibility.

Compost / Mulch

Benefit: Improves soil structure, moisture retention and microbial health, reducing disease pressure.

Application: Apply 2-4 inches of compost annually and mulch to conserve moisture.

Potassium (K) Boost

Benefit: Enhances fruit quality and stress tolerance; useful for reducing disease severity.

Application: Apply SOP or MOP per soil test; foliar K sprays for quick correction.

Calcium / Magnesium

Benefit: Prevents blossom end rot and strengthens tissues to resist infection.

Application: Use gypsum, lime or foliar Ca/Mg sprays based on soil tests.

Micronutrient Mix

Benefit: Corrects trace element deficiencies and improves overall plant health.

Application: Apply foliar or soil-applied chelates as recommended by soil tests.
