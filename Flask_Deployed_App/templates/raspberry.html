🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Raspberry

Botrytis (Gray Mold)

Description: Gray fuzzy mold on fruit and flowers under cool, wet conditions.

Remedy: Improve air circulation, remove infected fruit, and apply protective fungicides during bloom and harvest.

Anthracnose

Description: Small sunken lesions on canes and fruit leading to fruit rot and dieback.

Remedy: Prune diseased canes, maintain sanitation, and apply recommended fungicides when needed.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery coating on leaves causing reduced vigor and yield.

Remedy: Improve airflow, remove affected tissue, and apply sulfur-based treatments when necessary.

Cane Blight

Description: Sunken, discolored lesions on canes leading to dieback and reduced fruiting.

Remedy: Prune out affected canes, avoid excessive nitrogen, and apply fungicides if outbreak occurs.

Phytophthora / Root Rot

Description: Poor vigor, wilting and root decay in poorly drained soils.

Remedy: Improve drainage, plant on raised beds, and avoid overwatering; use resistant rootstocks where available.

Rust

Description: Orange or yellow pustules on undersides of leaves causing defoliation in severe cases.

Remedy: Remove and destroy infected leaves, improve airflow, and consider fungicide applications during wet weather.

Spur Blight

Description: Small necrotic spots on leaves and tips of shoots leading to shoot dieback.

Remedy: Prune and burn affected tips, maintain good sanitation, and apply protectant fungicides if necessary.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Balanced NPK (10-10-10)

Purpose: Supports overall plant growth and fruit development.

Application: Apply in early spring and following harvest according to soil test.

Phosphorus Boost

Purpose: Promotes strong root development and flower/fruit set.

Application: Apply bone meal or rock phosphate when preparing beds if soil tests low.

Organic Compost / Mulch

Benefit: Improves soil structure, microbial activity and moisture retention reducing disease pressure.

Application: Add a 2-4 inch layer of compost or well-rotted manure annually.

Potassium (K) Supplement

Benefit: Boosts fruit quality and increases disease tolerance under stress.

Application: Apply muriate of potash or sulfate of potash according to recommendations; foliar K sprays for quick correction.

Calcium / Magnesium

Benefit: Prevents physiological disorders and improves cell wall strength, reducing susceptibility to rot.

Application: Apply lime, gypsum, or foliar calcium formulations and magnesium sulfate (Epsom salt) as needed.

Micronutrient Mix (Trace Elements)

Benefit: Supplies Fe, Zn, Mn, B and Cu for balanced plant nutrition and improved disease resistance.

Application: Use foliar micronutrient sprays or soil-applied chelates per soil test recommendations.
