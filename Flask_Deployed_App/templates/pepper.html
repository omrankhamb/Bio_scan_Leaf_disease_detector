🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Pepper Bell

Bacterial Spot

Description: Water-soaked spots on leaves and fruit that become dark and scabby.

Remedy: Use copper sprays, remove infected debris, and practice crop rotation.

Anthracnose

Description: Sunken, dark lesions on fruit and stems, often in wet conditions.

Remedy: Remove affected fruits, apply fungicides, and avoid overhead irrigation.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery growth on leaves, reducing photosynthesis and vigor.

Remedy: Apply sulfur or potassium bicarbonate, improve air flow and remove affected growth.

Phytophthora Blight

Description: Root and crown rot causing wilting and fruit rot in waterlogged soils.

Remedy: Improve drainage, avoid overwatering, and use tolerant rootstocks.

Mosaic Virus

Description: Mottled yellow/green leaf patterns, stunted growth; virus transmitted by aphids.

Remedy: Remove infected plants, control aphid vectors, and use resistant varieties when available.

Verticillium Wilt

Description: Wilting and yellowing due to soil-borne fungus; vascular discoloration present.

Remedy: Use crop rotation, remove infected debris, and select tolerant varieties.

Alternaria Leaf Spot

Description: Brown or black lesions on leaves and fruit; can cause defoliation.

Remedy: Remove infected tissue, apply fungicides, and practice good sanitation.

Target Spot

Description: Circular leaf spots with concentric rings leading to defoliation in humid conditions.

Remedy: Apply appropriate fungicides and reduce leaf wetness.

🥗 Common Fertilizers & Supplements

Balanced NPK (10-10-10)

Purpose: Supplies N-P-K for vegetative growth and fruit development.

Application: Broadcast or side-dress in early spring and again mid-season per label.

Compost / Organic Matter

Benefit: Improves soil structure, microbial activity, and water holding capacity.

Recommendation: Apply a 2–4 inch layer as top-dress or incorporate into beds annually.

Calcium Spray (Ca)

Benefit: Helps prevent blossom end rot and improves fruit firmness and shelf life.

Application: Apply as foliar sprays during active fruit growth; follow manufacturer rates.

Epsom Salt (MgSO4)

Benefit: Provides magnesium for chlorophyll production, can correct magnesium deficiency.

Application: Dissolve in water and apply as foliar spray or soil drench following recommended rates.

Humic / Fulvic Acid

Benefit: Improves nutrient uptake and soil microbial activity, helps plant stress tolerance.

Application: Apply as soil drench or foliar application as per product instructions.

Foliar Micronutrient Spray (B, Zn, Mn)

Benefit: Quickly corrects micronutrient deficiencies that affect leaf health and fruit set.

Application: Use as a foliar spray following the manufacturer's dilution and timing recommendations.
