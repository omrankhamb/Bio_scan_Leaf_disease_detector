This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

corn

Gray Leaf Spot

Description: Gray-brown rectangular lesions on corn leaves with dark borders.

Remedy: Use resistant varieties, crop rotation, fungicide sprays, and improve air circulation.

Northern Corn Leaf Blight

Description: Long, narrow tan lesions on leaves with darker borders spreading rapidly.

Remedy: Plant resistant hybrids, timely fungicide applications, and proper crop rotation.

Southern Corn Leaf Blight

Description: Eye-shaped lesions with tan centers and dark reddish borders on leaves.

Remedy: Use resistant varieties, crop rotation, remove plant debris, and apply fungicides.

Common Rust

Description: Reddish-brown pustules on both leaf surfaces of corn plants.

Remedy: Plant resistant hybrids, apply fungicides when needed, and maintain proper spacing.

Maize Dwarf Mosaic

Description: Mosaic mottling, leaf streaking, and stunted growth of corn plants.

Remedy: Use resistant varieties, control aphid vectors, remove infected plants, and practice crop rotation.

Corn Smut

Description: Large black/gray galls on ears, tassels, stalks, and leaves.

Remedy: Use resistant varieties, remove infected plant parts, practice crop rotation, and maintain hygiene.

Anthracnose

Description: Dark, sunken lesions on stalks and leaves causing wilting and stalk rot.

Remedy: Use resistant hybrids, improve air circulation, crop rotation, and remove infected debris.

Stewart's Wilt

Description: Yellowing, wilting of leaves with water-soaked streaks on corn plants.

Remedy: Use resistant varieties, control beetle vectors, and practice crop rotation.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Folate (Vitamin B9)

Benefit: Essential for cell division, DNA synthesis, and fetal development.

Recommendation: One cup corn provides 76mcg folate; important for pregnancy and red blood cell formation.

Lutein & Zeaxanthin

Benefit: Carotenoids that protect eye health, reduce macular degeneration risk.

Recommendation: Yellow corn rich in these compounds; eat 1-2 servings weekly for eye health.

Urea Fertilizer (46-0-0)

Purpose: High nitrogen content promotes vigorous vegetative growth in corn.

Application: Apply 100-150 lbs/acre split at planting and V4-V6 growth stage (4-6 leaves).

Ammonium Nitrate (34-0-0)

Purpose: Quick nitrogen source, promotes leaf and stalk development in corn.

Application: Apply 50-75 lbs/acre at V6-V8 stage; water in for faster absorption.

Zinc Sulfate

Purpose: Micronutrient for enzyme function, prevents zinc deficiency in corn.

Application: Spray 0.5-1 lb/acre at V4-V6 stage; or soil apply 10-20 lbs/acre.

Potassium Chloride (0-0-60)

Purpose: Essential for grain fill, disease resistance, and yield in corn.

Application: Apply 40-60 lbs/acre; improves stalks strength and reduces lodging.
