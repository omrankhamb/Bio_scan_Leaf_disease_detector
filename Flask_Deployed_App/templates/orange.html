🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Orange

Citrus Canker

Description: Raised, corky lesions on leaves and fruit; highly infectious bacterial disease.

Remedy: Remove infected tissue, apply copper sprays, and avoid moving infected plant material.

Citrus Greening (HLB)

Description: Mottled leaves, misshapen fruit, and tree decline caused by Candidatus Liberibacter spp.

Remedy: Remove infected trees, control psyllid vectors, and replant with disease-free stock.

Alternaria Brown Spot

Description: Brown necrotic spots on leaves and fruit, often worsened by humidity.

Remedy: Prune for airflow, remove infected debris, and apply fungicides when necessary.

Greasy Spot

Description: Yellowish oily-looking spots on leaves caused by the fungus Mycosphaerella citri.

Remedy: Apply fungicides in the growing season and remove fallen infected leaves.

Anthracnose

Description: Dark lesions on fruit and twigs, often in wet conditions causing fruit drop and blemishes.

Remedy: Remove affected fruit, apply fungicides, and maintain good orchard hygiene.

Phytophthora Root Rot

Description: Root decay and poor vigor in poorly drained soils caused by Phytophthora spp.

Remedy: Improve drainage, avoid waterlogging, and use tolerant rootstocks where available.

Sooty Mold

Description: Black sooty coating on leaves and fruit resulting from honeydew of pests like aphids.

Remedy: Control sap-sucking pests, wash fruit if necessary, and improve natural predator presence.

Scale / Mite Infestation

Description: Sap-sucking pests that cause leaf chlorosis, drop and reduced vigor.

Remedy: Apply horticultural oils, insecticidal soaps and encourage beneficial insects.

🥗 Common Fertilizers & Supplements

DAP (High Phosphorus)

Purpose: Phosphorus for root establishment and early growth.

Application: Apply at planting or per soil test recommendations.

Balanced NPK (10-10-10)

Purpose: General purpose nutrition for tree growth and fruiting.

Application: Apply according to tree age and label instructions.

Potassium Boost (5-10-5)

Purpose: Improve fruit size, quality and stress tolerance.

Application: Use during fruit development as foliar spray or soil application per label.

Seaweed Extract

Benefit: Natural plant stimulant to improve stress tolerance and micronutrients.

Application: Apply as foliar feed during active growth.

Boron Supplement

Purpose: Supports fruit set and prevents boron-related disorders.

Application: Apply only if soil test indicates deficiency; follow label rates.

Calcium Spray

Benefit: Prevents disorders and improves fruit quality.

Application: Foliar spray during fruit development as needed.
