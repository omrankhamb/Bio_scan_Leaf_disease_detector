🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Apple

Apple Scab

Description: Brown or olive-colored lesions on leaves and fruit.

Remedy: Use fungicide sprays, remove infected leaves, improve air circulation, and avoid overhead watering.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery coating on leaves, stems, and fruit.

Remedy: Apply sulfur or potassium bicarbonate fungicides, prune affected areas, and ensure good air flow.

Cedar Apple Rust

Description: Yellow-orange spots with red borders on leaves.

Remedy: Remove galls from nearby cedar trees, apply fungicides in spring, and select resistant varieties.

Leaf Spot (Early Blight)

Description: Small brown spots with concentric rings on leaves.

Remedy: Remove infected leaves, apply copper fungicides, maintain hygiene, and improve drainage.

Sooty Blotch

Description: Dark gray or black smudge-like spots on fruit and leaves.

Remedy: Improve air circulation, apply fungicides, remove dead wood, and maintain proper spacing.

Black Rot

Description: Large black or brown lesions on apple fruit with concentric rings and fruit rot.

Remedy: Remove infected fruit and cankers, prune affected branches, apply fungicides, and improve air flow.

Fire Blight

Description: Shoots wilt and turn black, blossoms blight, and cankers form on branches.

Remedy: Prune affected branches, disinfect tools, avoid nitrogen fertilizer, and use resistant varieties.

Rust

Description: Yellow-orange pustules on leaf undersides causing early leaf drop.

Remedy: Remove affected leaves, apply sulfur or copper fungicides, and maintain good air circulation.

Alternaria Leaf Spot

Description: Dark concentric rings on leaves resembling a target pattern with yellowing halo.

Remedy: Remove infected leaves, apply copper or sulfur fungicides, improve air circulation, and prune lower branches.

Flyspeck

Description: Small dark, sooty spots clustered on apple fruit surface affecting appearance.

Remedy: Improve air circulation, apply fungicides in early season, and maintain orchard hygiene.

Fruit Crack

Description: Radial cracks on apple fruit skin from irregular watering and temperature stress.

Remedy: Maintain consistent soil moisture, provide shade cloth during heat, and avoid over-fertilizing.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Quercetin

Benefit: Flavonoid antioxidant reduces inflammation and supports immune function.

Recommendation: One apple provides 4.7mg quercetin; eat daily for anti-inflammatory benefits.

Fiber (Pectin)

Benefit: Soluble fiber aids digestion, lowers cholesterol, stabilizes blood sugar.

Recommendation: Eat apples with skin; 1 medium apple provides 4.4g fiber (17% daily value).

Balanced NPK (15-15-15)

Purpose: Complete fertilizer for overall apple tree growth and fruit development.

Application: Apply 3-5 oz per tree in early spring; follow with liquid fertilizer every 3 weeks.

Boron

Benefit: Essential micronutrient for bone health and hormone regulation.

Recommendation: Apples are good boron source; combine with magnesium-rich foods for better absorption.

Borax or Boron Fertilizer (0.5-2% B)

Purpose: Corrects boron deficiency, improves pollination and fruit set in apples.

Application: Spray 0.1-0.2% solution during bloom; prevents misshapen fruits and poor flowering.

Zinc Sulfate

Purpose: Micronutrient for enzyme function, improves fruit quality and disease resistance.

Application: Apply 1-2 oz per tree in spring; foliar spray 0.05% during growing season.
