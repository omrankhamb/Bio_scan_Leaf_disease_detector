🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Peach

Peach Leaf Curl

Description: Distorted, puckered leaves with reddish blisters caused by Taphrina deformans.

Remedy: Apply preventative fungicide sprays in late autumn or early spring and remove infected leaves.

Brown Rot

Description: Fungal infection causing fruit rot and blossom blight; brown, soft fruit lesions.

Remedy: Remove mummified fruit, apply fungicides at blossom and pre-harvest, and prune for airflow.

Leaf Spot

Description: Small dark lesions on leaves that can coalesce and cause defoliation.

Remedy: Remove affected leaves, apply appropriate fungicides, and avoid overhead watering.

Bacterial Spot

Description: Water-soaked spots on leaves and fruit that turn brown and scabby.

Remedy: Use copper sprays, remove infected tissue, and use certified disease-free stock.

Coryneum Blight (Shot Hole)

Description: Lesions that cause small holes in leaves and scabs on fruit.

Remedy: Prune out infected twigs, apply fungicide sprays in winter and spring.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery coating on leaves, shoots and buds reducing vigor.

Remedy: Apply sulfur or potassium bicarbonate, improve airflow, and remove affected growth.

Twig Canker

Description: Sunken areas on twigs and branches leading to dieback.

Remedy: Prune out cankered wood, disinfect tools, and maintain tree vigor with good nutrition.

Scale Infestation

Description: Small, round/sessile insects on stems and leaves causing yellowing and reduced vigor.

Remedy: Prune heavily infested branches, apply horticultural oil or insecticidal soap, and encourage natural predators.

Melanose

Description: Small dark brown lesions on leaves and fruit caused by a fungal pathogen.

Remedy: Prune for good airflow, remove infected debris, and apply fungicides if necessary.

Fruit Rot (Phytophthora)

Description: Soft, rotting areas on fruit often associated with waterlogged conditions.

Remedy: Improve drainage, avoid prolonged wetting, and apply appropriate fungicides to roots if needed.

Gummosis / Canker

Description: Cankers on trunk and branches with gum exudation leading to dieback and reduced vigor.

Remedy: Prune infected wood, improve tree vigor, and apply appropriate protective sprays during dormant season.

🥗 Common Fertilizers & Supplements

Balanced NPK (10-10-10)

Purpose: General purpose fertilizer for growth and fruit production.

Application: Apply according to tree age and label instructions.

Compost / Organic Matter

Benefit: Improves soil structure and slow-release nutrition.

Recommendation: Apply annually around the root zone.

Micronutrient Mix (B, Zn, Mg)

Purpose: Prevents common micronutrient deficiencies that affect fruit set and quality.

Application: Foliar spray as needed following product guidance.

Seaweed Extract

Benefit: Natural growth stimulant rich in trace elements and hormones.

Application: Apply as foliar spray during active growth to improve stress tolerance.

Calcium Spray (Ca, B)

Benefit: Prevents blossom end rot and improves fruit firmness.

Application: Foliar apply during fruit development as per product instructions.

Slow-Release Organic NPK

Benefit: Provides steady nutrition over time without leaching.

Application: Apply granules around root zone in early spring following label rates.
