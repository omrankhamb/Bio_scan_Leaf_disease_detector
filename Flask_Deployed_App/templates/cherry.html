🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

cherry

Cherry Leaf Spot

Description: Small purple/brown spots on cherry leaves that enlarge and cause yellowing.

Remedy: Rake and destroy fallen leaves, apply fungicides in spring, and prune for air circulation.

Brown Rot

Description: Brown, sunken spots on cherry fruit and blossom blight affecting yield.

Remedy: Remove mummified fruit, prune for air flow, and apply fungicides at bloom and pre-harvest.

Bacterial Canker

Description: Sunken, oozing cankers on branches with wilting and dieback symptoms.

Remedy: Prune infected wood, disinfect tools, avoid wounding, and use copper sprays during dormancy.

Shot Hole

Description: Small round lesions on cherry leaves that fall out leaving 'shot holes'.

Remedy: Remove affected tissue, improve air circulation, and apply fungicide/bactericide sprays.

Gummosis

Description: Sap oozing from trunk or branches, often following wounds or infections.

Remedy: Avoid wounding trees, improve vigor, remove affected branches and treat infections.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery coating on cherry leaves, shoots, and fruit surface.

Remedy: Apply sulfur or potassium bicarbonate sprays, improve air circulation, and remove affected shoots.

Bacterial Spot

Description: Dark water-soaked spots on cherry leaves and fruit that coalesce.

Remedy: Use disease-free stock, apply copper sprays during wet periods, and increase air circulation.

Virus Symptoms

Description: Stunted growth, leaf mottling, and reduced yield from viral infections.

Remedy: Remove infected trees, control insect vectors, and use certified virus-free planting material.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Anthocyanins

Benefit: Powerful antioxidants giving cherries their red color, supports heart health.

Recommendation: Consume 1 cup of fresh cherries daily (60-100g) for optimal antioxidant intake.

Melatonin

Benefit: Natural sleep regulator, reduces inflammation and promotes better sleep quality.

Recommendation: Tart cherry juice or fresh cherries 1-2 hours before bedtime for sleep benefits.

NPK Fertilizer (10-10-10)

Purpose: Balanced fertilizer for overall cherry tree health, growth, and fruit production.

Application: Apply 2-4 oz per tree in spring; repeat every 6 weeks during growing season.

Potassium

Benefit: Regulates blood pressure, supports muscle function, prevents cramps.

Recommendation: One cup of cherries provides 9% daily potassium; ideal post-workout snack.

Muriate of Potash (0-0-60)

Purpose: High potassium content boosts fruit quality, size, and sweetness in cherries.

Application: Apply 1-2 oz per tree in late spring; increases sugar content in fruit.

Calcium Nitrate

Purpose: Prevents blossom-end rot, strengthens fruit structure, improves disease resistance.

Application: Spray 1% solution on leaves during fruiting stage; apply 2-3 times per season.
