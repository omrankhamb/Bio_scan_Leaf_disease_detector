Strawberry

Strawberry Leaf Spot

Description: Small round, purple to red spots on upper leaf surfaces often with yellow halos; common foliar fungal disease.

Remedy: Remove and destroy infected leaves, avoid overhead watering, ensure good spacing, and apply appropriate fungicides when needed.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery growth on leaves and stems, reducing photosynthesis and vigor.

Remedy: Increase air circulation, avoid high humidity, and apply sulfur or potassium bicarbonate treatments.

Gray Mold (Botrytis)

Description: Brown lesions and fuzzy gray spore masses on fruit and flowers, favored by wet conditions.

Remedy: Improve drainage, remove mummified fruit, avoid overhead irrigation, and apply fungicides at blossom and pre-harvest stages.

Angular Leaf Spot

Description: Water-soaked, angular lesions often at leaf margins caused by bacterial infection; may lead to leaf yellowing.

Remedy: Remove infected leaves, improve air circulation and drainage, avoid overhead irrigation, and apply copper-based bactericides if necessary.

Leaf Rust

Description: Orange to reddish pustules on leaf undersides causing premature leaf drop and reduced vigor.

Remedy: Remove heavily infected foliage, improve spacing and airflow, and apply appropriate fungicides when detected early.

Leaf Blight (Anthracnose)

Description: Dark, sunken lesions that may coalesce to cause extensive leaf blighting; also affects runners and crowns.

Remedy: Remove infected tissue, rotate cropping areas, avoid wet leaves, and use fungicide applications during disease-prone periods.

Crown Rot

Description: Water-soaked crown tissue that becomes soft and brown, leading to plant collapse; often from soil-borne fungi.

Remedy: Improve drainage, avoid overwatering, remove affected plants, and use disease-free planting material and fungicide drenches when appropriate.

Red Stele (Phytophthora)

Description: Root disease causing reddish-brown stele and poor vigor; plants show wilting and decline in cool, waterlogged soils.

Remedy: Improve soil drainage, avoid planting in infested fields, use tolerant varieties, and apply appropriate soil treatments where recommended.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Nearley 6

Purpose: Common foliar fertilizer for strawberries; formulated to support leaf health and fruit set.

Application: Apply according to label rates during early growth and pre-flowering for best results.

Balanced NPK (10-10-10)

Purpose: Supports overall plant growth and fruit development in strawberries.

Application: Apply as granular or liquid feed in early spring and after harvest as recommended.

Compost / Organic Matter

Benefit: Improves soil structure, water retention, and slow-release nutrients for healthier strawberry plants.

Recommendation: Apply a 1-2" layer of well-rotted compost annually before planting or in early spring.

Potassium Boost

Purpose: High-potassium feed to improve fruit size, sweetness and stress tolerance.

Application: Apply as a late-season feed or foliar spray according to product directions to enhance ripening.

Calcium & Magnesium (CaMg)

Purpose: Prevents tip burn and blossom-end rot; strengthens cell walls and improves uptake of other nutrients.

Application: Apply as a foliar spray during early fruit development and as soil amendments where deficiencies are known.

Micronutrient Mix (Trace Elements)

Purpose: Supplies essential trace elements (Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu) to correct deficiencies and support healthy foliage.

Application: Use as foliar or soil-applied treatments following soil test recommendations.
