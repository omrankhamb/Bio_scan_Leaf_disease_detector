🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Blue Cherry

Brown Rot

Description: Brown, sunken fruit spots and blossom blight caused by fungi.

Remedy: Remove mummified fruit, prune for air flow, apply recommended fungicides at bloom and pre-harvest.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery growth on leaves and shoots, may distort leaves.

Remedy: Prune affected shoots, improve ventilation, apply sulfur or potassium bicarbonate sprays.

Cherry Leaf Spot

Description: Small purple spots on leaves that enlarge and cause leaf drop.

Remedy: Rake and destroy fallen leaves, apply fungicides in spring, plant resistant varieties.

Bacterial Canker

Description: Sunken, oozing cankers on branches and trunk; dieback of shoots.

Remedy: Prune out infected wood, disinfect tools, avoid wounding, and use copper sprays in spring.

Shot Hole

Description: Small round holes in leaves and fruit, caused by fungi or bacteria.

Remedy: Remove and destroy affected tissue, apply fungicide/bactericide sprays as recommended.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery coating on cherry leaves, shoots, and fruit surface.

Remedy: Apply sulfur or potassium bicarbonate sprays, improve air circulation, remove affected shoots.

Fruit Rot

Description: Soft, mushy decay of cherry fruit with mold growth on surface.

Remedy: Remove infected fruit, improve air circulation, apply fungicides, maintain tree vigor.

Leaf Curl

Description: Curled, puckered leaves with reddish or purple discoloration.

Remedy: Apply dormant oil sprays before bud break, use fungicides, prune heavily infected branches.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Anthocyanins

Benefit: Powerful antioxidant pigments provide anti-inflammatory and cardiovascular benefits.

Recommendation: Blue cherries rich in anthocyanins; eat fresh or frozen for maximum antioxidant activity.

Melatonin

Benefit: Natural sleep hormone, powerful antioxidant, supports immune function.

Recommendation: Blue cherry juice before bedtime aids sleep; 1 cup provides significant melatonin levels.

NPK Fertilizer (10-10-10)

Purpose: Balanced blend for overall cherry tree growth, flowering, and fruit development.

Application: Apply 2-3 oz per tree in spring; repeat every 4-6 weeks during growing season.

Potassium

Benefit: Supports muscle function, heart health, and electrolyte balance.

Recommendation: One cup blue cherries provides 268mg potassium; beneficial post-workout.

Muriate of Potash (0-0-60)

Purpose: Pure potassium fertilizer enhances fruit flavor, color, and disease resistance in cherries.

Application: Apply 1-2 oz per tree at fruit development stage; improves yield and quality.

Calcium Nitrate

Purpose: Provides calcium for strong cell walls, nitrogen for leaf growth, prevents fruit crack.

Application: Foliar spray 0.5-1% during fruit development; 1-2 oz per tree every 2 weeks.
