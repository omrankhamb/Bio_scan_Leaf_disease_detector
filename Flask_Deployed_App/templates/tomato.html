🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Tomato

Tomato Leaf Spot

Description: Brown to black circular lesions on leaves and fruit; common fungal disease that reduces yield.

Remedy: Remove infected material, rotate crops, avoid overhead irrigation, and apply fungicides as needed.

Early Blight

Description: Concentric dark rings on lower leaves leading to defoliation and reduced fruit set.

Remedy: Remove lower leaves, mulch to prevent soil splash, and apply copper or chlorothalonil-based fungicides.

Late Blight

Description: Rapidly spreading water-soaked lesions on leaves and stems; caused by Phytophthora infestans.

Remedy: Remove infected plants, apply appropriate fungicides, and avoid wet foliage conditions.

Septoria Leaf Spot

Description: Small circular spots with gray centers and dark borders on lower leaves.

Remedy: Remove affected foliage, practice crop rotation, and use fungicide sprays if needed.

Bacterial Spot

Description: Small dark lesions that may coalesce on leaves and fruit; caused by Xanthomonas spp.

Remedy: Use certified disease-free seed, copper sprays, and avoid working plants when wet.

Verticillium Wilt

Description: Yellowing and wilting of leaves often starting on one side of the plant; soil-borne fungus.

Remedy: Rotate crops, remove infected plants, and use resistant varieties.

Fusarium Wilt

Description: Wilting and yellowing that progresses to plant death; caused by Fusarium oxysporum in soil.

Remedy: Use resistant varieties, practice crop rotation, and improve soil health; remove and destroy infected plants.

Tomato Mosaic Virus

Description: Mottling, distortion and stunting of foliage and fruit; spread mechanically and by contaminated tools.

Remedy: Use virus-free seed/stock, sanitize tools, remove infected plants, and control vectors where applicable.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Nearley 6 (Tomato Leaf Fertilizer)

Purpose: Concentrated foliar formula to support leaf health, fruit set, and stress resilience in tomato plants.

Application: Apply as directed on label during vegetative growth and pre-flowering; repeat as needed following recommendations.

DAP (High Phosphorus)

Purpose: Diammonium phosphate provides a phosphorus boost to promote root development and early establishment.

Application: Apply at planting or as recommended by soil test; follow dilution and safety instructions on product label.

Balanced NPK (15-15-15)

Purpose: Even nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium to support balanced vegetative growth and fruiting.

Application: Use as a base fertilizer in early season and follow with targeted feeds during fruiting.

Potassium Boost (5-10-5)

Purpose: Higher potassium feed to improve fruit size, flavor and stress tolerance during ripening.

Application: Apply as a late-season supplement or foliar spray per label to enhance ripening and firmness.

Tomato Food (Specialized Mix)

Purpose: Formulated for tomatoes (often slightly higher P and K) to support flowering, fruit set and flavor development.

Application: Follow manufacturer instructions for timing and rates; often applied during flowering and fruit set.

Calcium & Magnesium (CaMg)

Purpose: Prevents blossom-end rot and strengthens fruit quality.

Application: Foliar or soil application based on tissue/soil tests.
