🍁Plant Disease Detection🍁

This AI Engine Will Help To Detect Disease From Following Fruites And Veggies

Potato

Late Blight

Description: Water-soaked spots on leaves that expand to brown/black lesions; caused by Phytophthora infestans.

Remedy: Remove infected material, use certified seed, and apply recommended fungicides during wet periods.

Early Blight

Description: Dark brown spots with concentric rings on older leaves; favored by wet conditions.

Remedy: Rotate crops, remove debris, avoid overhead irrigation, and apply fungicides when necessary.

Septoria Leaf Spot

Description: Small dark spots on lower leaves that can lead to defoliation under wet conditions.

Remedy: Remove affected leaves, practice crop rotation, and use fungicides if outbreaks occur.

Powdery Mildew

Description: White powdery growth on upper leaf surfaces reducing photosynthesis.

Remedy: Improve airflow, remove infected leaves, and apply sulfur or bicarbonate-based treatments.

Bacterial Wilt / Blackleg

Description: Wilting, stem blackening and rot commonly spread via tubers and infected soil.

Remedy: Use certified disease-free seed, remove infected plants, and improve soil drainage.

Mosaic / PVY

Description: Mottled or mosaic leaf patterns, stunting and reduced yields; often vectored by aphids.

Remedy: Rogue infected plants, control aphid vectors, and plant virus-free seed.

Leaf Roll Virus

Description: Leaves roll upward and plants show reduced vigor; virus-transmitted.

Remedy: Remove infected plants, control aphids, and use certified seed.

Aphid / Sucking Pest Damage

Description: Distorted leaves, honeydew and sooty mold caused by sap-sucking insects leading to secondary disease.

Remedy: Encourage predators, use insecticidal soap or horticultural oils, and monitor regularly.

🥗 Nutritional Supplements & Fertilizers

Balanced NPK

Purpose: Supplies essential N-P-K to support foliage and tuber development and improve plant vigor against disease.

Application: Apply based on soil test; split applications reduce disease susceptibility.

Potassium (K) Boost

Benefit: Improves stress tolerance and disease resistance, notably reducing severity of some blights.

Application: Apply potash as side-dress or foliar sprays per recommendations.

Calcium / Magnesium

Benefit: Strengthens cell walls and reduces physiological disorders that predispose to infection.

Application: Apply as foliar sprays or soil amendments according to soil tests.
